Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 17, 2026
This Privacy Policy applies to YarnCake, a yarn and knitting inventory application (the "App"). The App is local-first: by default all your data stays on your device. There are no accounts, no social features, and no advertising or analytics tracking. We comply with applicable data protection laws, including the U.S. App Store privacy requirements and, where applicable, the Personal Information Protection Law of the People's Republic of China.
By downloading, installing or using the App, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please stop using and uninstall the App.

1. Data Stored on Your Device

Core features — your yarn stash, projects, patterns, tools, toolbox calculations, and settings — run locally and are stored in a database on your device. We do not require you to sign in, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any account credentials.

2. Photos You Choose to Add

To attach a cover image to a yarn, project, or pattern swatch, the App asks for your permission to use the camera or photo library. Photos you take or select are used only as covers inside the App and are stored locally on your device. The App does not access your photo library in the background, does not upload your photos to our servers, and has no server of its own.

3. Optional iCloud Backup (Pro Feature)

If you are a Pro subscriber and you actively choose to enable iCloud backup, the App creates an encrypted snapshot of your local data — including your records and the cover photos you added — and uploads it to your own private CloudKit database under your Apple ID (container iCloud.com.canxikeji.yarnstash). This backup is stored in Apple's iCloud, is accessible only to your Apple account, and is used solely to restore your data across your own devices. It is never shared with us or any third party. You can stop using this feature at any time; the App works fully without it. If you do not enable iCloud backup, no data ever leaves your device.

4. In-App Purchases

Optional Pro subscriptions and the lifetime unlock are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment information. Purchase and subscription records are managed by Apple under its own privacy policy.

5. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect advertising identifiers, location, contacts, browsing histories, usage analytics, or behavioral profiles. The App contains no third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. No personal data is transmitted to any server operated by the developer.

6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties. The only external services the App contacts — Apple App Store (StoreKit, for purchases) and Apple iCloud (CloudKit, only if you enable backup) — are operated by Apple and governed by Apple's terms. We may disclose information only where required by valid legal process.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is a general productivity tool for crafting hobbyists and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your Rights and Data Control

You own all your data. You can delete, clear, or uninstall the App and its local data at any time without contacting us. If you enabled iCloud backup, you can delete the backup record from your own iCloud account. To request any other data-related assistance, use the support contact provided in the App.

9. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the App or in legal requirements. Updates take effect when posted within the App. Continued use of the App constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the support email address provided in the App.
